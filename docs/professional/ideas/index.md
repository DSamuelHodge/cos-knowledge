## Description
Design explorations and reviews for the CoS operating system — frameworks,
architectures, and proposals under consideration.

## Purpose
Hold design work in the same searchable, contract-typed space as everything
else, so ideas are reviewable from anywhere and traceable to decisions.

## Content overview
One page per design artifact or review, prefixed `flue-`/`co-s-` for scanning.
Drafts carry `status: draft` until reviewed and adopted.

## Formatting guidelines
Standard page contract (id/type/category/updated/status/tags/tools). Link
related artifacts to each other; keep statuses honest (`draft` → `current` on
decision).
