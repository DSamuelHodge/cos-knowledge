Derrick Hodge — unified personal & professional knowledge base.

A Markdown folder committed to GitHub, built with Blume, served at
cos.hodgederrick.com. Two root categories — `personal/` and `professional/`
(see sidebar). Every section is a standardized page: description, purpose,
content overview, formatting guidelines, recommended tools; and every page
carries a strict frontmatter contract (`id`, `type`, `category`, `updated`,
`tags`) so the tree stays parseable into a knowledge graph.

## Sections

### Personal
- [People](personal/people/) — relationships and per-person context
- [Health](personal/health/) — wellbeing and care continuity
- [Finances](personal/finances/) — budgets, bills, obligations
- [Home](personal/home/) — home and vehicle operations
- [Learning](personal/learning/) — courses, books, takeaways
- [Goals](personal/goals/) — goals and habit routines
- [Travel](personal/travel/) — trips and itineraries

### Professional
